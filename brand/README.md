# Lock Your brand mark

The primary symbol is the **quiet orbit**: an open warm-orange enclosure holding two calm pause bars. It is intentionally language-neutral and remains legible at favicon size.

## Files

- `lockphone-mark-v1.svg` / `.png` — transparent mark for light backgrounds
- `lockphone-mark-on-dark-v1.svg` / `.png` — transparent mark for dark backgrounds
- `lockphone-app-icon-v1.svg` / `.png` — opaque 1024×1024 iOS and social icon
- `previous/` — the product icons replaced by this version
- `explorations/` — retained ImageGen concept renders

## Palette

- Warm vermilion: `#F06A3B`
- Deep charcoal: `#151513`
- Warm ivory: `#FFF4E8`

Keep clear space of at least one pause-bar width around the mark. Do not add text inside it, rotate it, stretch it, or apply gradients and shadows.
